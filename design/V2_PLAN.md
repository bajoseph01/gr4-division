# Gr4 Division V2

Approved reference: `v2-concept.png`, accepted by Mr Jo on 7 November 2026.

## Purpose and acceptance

Teacher-led Grade 4 division on iPad/IWB, with solid coloured numeral tiles making each division round and brought-down digit traceable. Preserve custom problems, 20 long/four short samples, step checking, reveal/reset and scratchpad. Keep ÷ notation and offline/direct-file use. The partially completed reference is 7372 ÷ 3 with quotient 24 and next working number 17; future answers stay hidden.

## Implementation map

- Header, visible mode tabs, problem banner, teacher drawer and prompt card: semantic HTML and `styles.css`, using the approved warm ivory/deep green palette.
- Tiles, bracket, quotient, subtraction lines and working rows: CSS grids generated by `board.js`, sharing exact digit columns.
- Bring-down arrows: local inline SVG positioned from actual source/destination tile rectangles, redrawn after rendering and resizing.
- Teaching sequence and scratchpad: existing JavaScript in `index.html`; Canvas remains the drawing surface.
- No external assets, new libraries, runtime generation, accounts or CDN. Original approved image remains a design reference, not an app background.

## Colour rules

Colour identifies a division round, not strict place value. Orange → green → yellow → blue → lavender, repeating only for problems longer than five rounds. Quotient digit, product and remainder use that round's colour. A brought-down digit keeps its source/next-round colour. Leading dividend digits grouped in the first operation share its colour. Divisor coral, final answer remainder purple. Named steps and active outline supplement colour.

## Deliberate practical details

Fresh opening starts with 48 ÷ 2 in both modes. The four-digit image is a visual reference only. Guided long division has five levels: two-digit exact without carrying, two-digit exact with carrying, two-digit remainders, three-digit exact, then three-digit remainders. Next problem is enabled after completion; a separate Next level click moves up. Teacher-selected/custom problems remain available. Working in words is collapsible. Zero is displayed explicitly rather than as a dot. Actual renders use system-installed Trebuchet/Verdana for offline legibility; tiles are flat colour rather than generated shading. Portrait/mobile require normal vertical scrolling. Complete four-digit working can also exceed a landscape iPad's height. Short division retains its established compact notation. No publication is included.
